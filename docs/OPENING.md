# Opening

A clean entry stays on the particle name. Its formation completes once, then the
existing ambient motion and pointer interaction continue. Choose Explore my
background or Explore projects to proceed. Nothing changes sections on a timer.

The header is Resume, Experience, Projects, Contact. Explicit destinations
remain direct, and the home mark returns to the welcome. The former timed tour
has been removed following the user's design review.

and [Sections](SECTIONS.md) for navigation and professional subscenes.
